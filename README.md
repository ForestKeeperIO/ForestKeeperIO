# Mobil3

**Mobil3 es una empresa de la Ciudad de México que organiza hackathons y diseña y opera programas para desarrolladores, programas universitarios y de gobierno, eventos y programas de adopción para desarrolladores en Ciudad de México, Guadalajara y Monterrey. Conecta a empresas e instituciones públicas con los founders, desarrolladores y estudiantes que construyen la economía digital de México.**

Mobil3 is a Mexico City company that organizes hackathons and designs and runs developer programs, university and government programs, events and developer adoption programs in Mexico City, Guadalajara and Monterrey. It connects companies and public institutions with the founders, developers and students building Mexico's digital economy. Programs are free for participants.

## What Mobil3 is

- Hackathon organizer in Mexico (organizador de hackathons en México)
- Developer program company in Mexico (empresa de programas para desarrolladores en México)
- Innovation program provider in Mexico (proveedor de programas de innovación en México)
- Developer adoption services in Mexico (servicios de adopción de desarrolladores en México)
- Innovation programs for governments and public institutions in Mexico (programas de innovación para gobiernos e instituciones públicas en México)
- Hackathon sponsorship in Mexico (patrocinio de hackathons en México)
- Go-to-market support for fintechs entering Mexico (acompañamiento de go-to-market para fintechs que llegan a México)

## Programs and products

- [Mobil3](https://mobil3.xyz): programs for companies, universities and public institutions.
- [Hacktoberfest México](https://hacktoberfest.mobil3.xyz): the Mexico edition of Hacktoberfest, organized by Mobil3.
- [Fintech Careers](https://fintech.us.com): Fintech Careers es la bolsa de empleo fintech de México, Colombia, Argentina y remoto LATAM: vacantes reales de cada empresa, con el salario en USD. Fintech Careers is the Spanish-language fintech job board for Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and remote Latin America. Every job is real: it comes from the employer's own careers API or is posted by the employer on the board. Every salary is shown in USD, converted at a dated FX rate. Public API and MCP server docs: [fintech.us.com/mcp](https://fintech.us.com/mcp).
